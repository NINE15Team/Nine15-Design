How the mobile experience works

Today on phones, the cow shows 21 small dots and each tap jumps straight into a product pop-up. The new version groups the cuts into 7 areas that are easy to tap. Customers can then look through every cut in an area before choosing one.

  1. 1

    See the whole animal

    The cow illustration runs full width on a cream panel, cropped to fill the screen. It carries 7 gold markers, one per area, in the same style as the desktop hotspots. They replace the 21 tiny dots.

  2. 2

    Tap the cow, or tap a name

    Every marker is a full-size touch target. Below the cow, the same areas appear as rounded, labelled tiles in a single row you can swipe, so they take up one line instead of a tall grid. The chosen area turns gold on the cow, and its tile turns white with a check mark.

  3. 3

    Browse that area's cuts

    The panel below changes to show the area name, a one-line description and its cuts. Under that is a row of product cards you can swipe, each with photo, name, price and Quick add. Quick add opens the same pop-up the site already uses.

  4. 4

    Keep exploring without scrolling back up

    Arrow buttons step to the next or previous area. The cards scroll sideways, so the section stays the same height and the page doesn't jump when the area changes.

The 7 areas (for approval)

All 22 products already on the desktop cow appear here, with the same names, photos and prices. The area descriptions are draft copy for you to confirm.

What stays the same

  • The desktop cow and its hover pop-ups are unchanged.
  • Same illustration, products, prices and Quick add flow.
  • Brand type, charcoal and gold palette. Buttons and product cards keep the site's square corners.
  • The new layout applies on phones only (below 750px wide).

Notes for review

  • Rib opens first by default. Any area can be the default instead.
  • Sold-out cuts would show the site's existing "Sold out" label in place of Quick add.
  • This is a concept preview. Quick add and product links are not live here.
